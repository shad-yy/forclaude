NOW restructured its sports memberships in October 2026, replacing the single Sports Membership with three separate tiers: Football at £23.99 per month, Multi-Sport at £19.99 per month, and All Sports at £27.99 per month. This guide breaks down exactly what each tier includes, how they compare to Sky Sports pricing, and whether IPTV alternatives offer better value.

## The New NOW Sports Tiers Explained

NOW (formerly NOW TV) previously offered a single Sports Membership granting access to all Sky Sports channels. In October 2026, this was replaced with a tiered system requiring customers to choose between three distinct packages.

| NOW Membership | Monthly Cost | Channels Included | Key Sports |
|----------------|-------------|-------------------|------------|
| Football | £23.99/mo | Sky Sports Premier League, Sky Sports Football | Premier League, EFL, Scottish Premiership |
| Multi-Sport | £19.99/mo | Sky Sports Main Event, F1, Cricket, Golf, Tennis, Action | Formula 1, Cricket, Golf, Tennis, Darts |
| All Sports | £27.99/mo | All Sky Sports channels | All of the above combined |

### What Changed

Under the previous structure, NOW Sports Membership customers paid a single fee (approximately £25/mo) and received access to all Sky Sports channels. The new system forces a choice: either pay for football coverage only, non-football sports only, or pay a premium for everything.

Customers who previously watched both Premier League football and Formula 1, for example, now face two options: subscribe to All Sports at £27.99 per month (a price increase) or purchase Football and Multi-Sport separately at a combined £43.98 per month.

### Free Alternatives Before You Subscribe

Before considering any paid sports subscription, UK viewers should be aware of the free options available. BBC and ITV broadcast major events including the FA Cup final, the European Championship, the World Cup, Wimbledon finals, and the Olympics free of charge. BBC Match of the Day provides comprehensive Premier League highlights every Saturday evening at no cost on BBC One and BBC iPlayer. Live radio commentary is free on BBC Radio 5 Live and talkSPORT.

NOW, Sky Sports, and IPTV services provide value for live match broadcasts that are not available free of charge. If you only follow highlights and major free-to-air events, you may not need any subscription at all.

## Who Should Choose NOW?

NOW memberships still serve a specific audience:

- **NOW Football (£23.99/mo):** Suitable for viewers who only want Sky Sports Premier League coverage and do not need Champions League, boxing, or entertainment services. NOW offers no-contract flexibility, meaning you can cancel month to month during the off-season.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does NOW Sports cost per month in October 2026?

NOW offers three sports tiers: Football at £23.99/mo, Multi-Sport at £19.99/mo, and All Sports at £27.99/mo. The previous single Sports Membership has been discontinued and replaced with this tiered structure.

### Can I watch the Champions League on NOW?

No. Champions League broadcast rights in the UK belong exclusively to TNT Sports, not Sky Sports. NOW memberships only include Sky Sports channels. You would need a separate TNT Sports subscription (£25/mo via discovery+) to watch the Champions League.

### Is NOW cheaper than Sky Sports?

NOW Football (£23.99/mo) is marginally cheaper than the Sky Sports add-on (£24/mo), but NOW does not require a broadband contract. However, NOW's All Sports tier (£27.99/mo) is more expensive than the Sky Sports add-on and still does not include TNT Sports or entertainment channels.

### What is the cheapest way to watch all Sky Sports channels?

NOW All Sports at £27.99 per month provides access to all Sky Sports channels without a contract. Smart Live TV includes all Sky Sports channels plus TNT Sports, DAZN, and entertainment from £12 per month.

### Does NOW include 3pm Premier League blackout matches?

No. NOW broadcasts Sky Sports channels, which are subject to the same UK 3pm Saturday blackout rules as all domestic broadcasters. Saturday 3pm Premier League matches are not available on NOW, Sky Sports, or any UK television provider. Smart Live TV provides access to these matches via international broadcast feeds.
