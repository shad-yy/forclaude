Every World Cup 2026 match is available on Amazon Firestick. 
Here is the complete guide — from the free BBC and ITV apps 
to getting Arabic commentary and 4K quality for every game.

## The Quick Answer

For UK viewers, the World Cup 2026 is completely free 
on Firestick using two apps:

- **BBC iPlayer app** — Available free from the Firestick App Store
- **ITVX app** — Available free from the Firestick App Store

All 104 matches are split between BBC and ITV. 
You need both apps installed to catch every game.

## Free Setup: BBC iPlayer + ITVX on Firestick

**Step 1** — Press the Home button on your Firestick remote  
**Step 2** — Go to Find → Search, type "BBC iPlayer"  
**Step 3** — Download and install the BBC iPlayer app (free)  
**Step 4** — Repeat for "ITVX" — download and install  
**Step 5** — Open BBC iPlayer, go to Sport → Football → World Cup  
**Step 6** — Create a free BBC account if prompted  
**Step 7** — Do the same in ITVX with a free ITV account

Both accounts are free. No credit card required.

You now have access to every World Cup 2026 match 
in the UK at no cost.

## What the Free Apps Do Not Give You

The BBC and ITV Firestick apps are excellent for most viewers. 
But there are four things they cannot provide:

**Arabic, French or Spanish commentary**  
BBC and ITV broadcast exclusively in English. 
beIN Sports Arabia, TF1 (French) and Movistar (Spanish) 
carry the same matches with local commentary. 
These channels are not available on standard UK apps.

**Guaranteed 4K quality**  
BBC iPlayer offers 4K for a selection of high-profile 
World Cup matches. ITVX streams in maximum HD. 
Not every game is available in 4K through the free apps.

**Reliable streaming during peak moments**  
BBC iPlayer and ITVX handle the entire UK national 
audience simultaneously. During England matches and 
high-profile knockouts, millions of viewers loading 
the same stream at the same moment causes buffering. 
This is a documented infrastructure issue, 
not a Firestick problem.

**Access outside the UK**  
Both apps are geoblocked. If your Firestick is 
outside the UK — travelling, living abroad, 
or visiting family — neither app works 
without a UK VPN.
