## Opening Weekend Shockers and Blackouts

The unprecedented 22-team expansion of the Premier League began with a bang. Defending champions Arsenal started strong with a 3-0 victory over newly-promoted Coventry City on Friday night. However, Saturday brought the real drama as Hull City stunned Manchester United 2-0 in the early kick-off.

Three critical matches today were entirely blacked out at 3pm, leaving supporters unable to watch them on Sky Sports or TNT Sports.
