## The Saturday 3pm Blackout Explained

Under UEFA Statute Article 48, the English Football Association prohibits live domestic football broadcasts between 14:45 and 17:15 on Saturday afternoons. This regulation, established in the 1960s to protect stadium attendances at lower-league clubs across the EFL and National League, prevents Sky Sports, TNT Sports, and all other UK broadcasters from televising Saturday 3pm matches.

As a result, five Matchweek 7 fixtures are completely unavailable on domestic television. Fans paying substantial monthly fees for official packages receive blank screens during the most popular kick-off window of the entire weekend.

Because international networks in North America (Peacock, USA Network), Australia (Optus Sport), and the Middle East (beIN Sports) are not subject to UK broadcast restrictions, every 3pm match is televised live overseas.
