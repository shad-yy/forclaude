## The Saturday 3pm Blackout Explained

Under UEFA Statute Article 48, the English Football Association enforces a broadcast blackout on all live football between 14:45 and 17:15 on Saturday afternoons. Designed in the 1960s to protect ticket sales and matchday attendances across lower divisions in the EFL and National League, the rule prevents domestic television companies like Sky Sports and TNT Sports from showing Saturday 3pm Premier League games.

As a result, five matches on Saturday, 5 September 2026—including Manchester City against Coventry City and Nottingham Forest against Tottenham Hotspur—are completely untelevised on UK television. Supporters who pay over £80 each month for official sports channels are left with blank screens during the busiest fixture window of the weekend.

Because international networks across the United States (Peacock, USA Network), Australia (Optus Sport), and the Middle East (beIN Sports) do not operate under UK broadcast regulations, every single 3pm fixture is broadcast live overseas.
