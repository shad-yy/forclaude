## Step-by-Step Anti-Buffering Configuration Guide

Even with a premium IPTV provider, local network issues can cause buffering. Follow these configuration steps to guarantee uninterrupted streaming:

### 1. Switch to a Wired Ethernet Connection
Wi-Fi is vulnerable to household interference and signal degradation. Use an Ethernet adapter to connect your Firestick or Smart TV directly to your router. A wired connection drops ping spikes and eliminates packet loss.

### 2. Configure Your Player Cache
Open your IPTV player settings (TiviMate or IPTV Smarters) and adjust the buffer size:
*   **Small (0–1 seconds):** Good for ultra-fast fibre connections.
*   **Medium (2–3 seconds):** Recommended for most connections to absorb minor speed dips.
*   **Large (5+ seconds):** Best if you experience frequent buffering, though it introduces a slight delay from live action.

### 3. Check Your ISP Speed
Verify you have the required download bandwidth:
*   **HD (1080p):** Minimum 10 Mbps.
*   **4K UHD (2160p):** Minimum 25 Mbps.

Test your local speed using a browser-based speed test directly on your streaming device.
