## Official UK TV Rights & Broadcasters for 2026-27

For the 2026-27 season, domestic live broadcasts remain divided between three primary rights holders.

### 1. Sky Sports
Sky Sports remains the dominant broadcaster, showing the majority of live matches.
*   **Fixtures:** 128 live matches per season.
*   **Kickoff Slots:** Friday nights, Saturday 5:30 PM, Sunday 2:00 PM & 4:30 PM, and selected Monday night games.
*   **Cost:** Standalone Sky Sports package starts at £43/month, often requiring bundled TV contracts.

### 2. TNT Sports (formerly BT Sport)
TNT Sports covers the early weekend kickoffs and midweek rounds.
*   **Fixtures:** 52 live matches.
*   **Kickoff Slots:** Saturday 12:30 PM and selected midweek fixtures.
*   **Cost:** £30.99/month via discovery+ or Sky/Virgin add-ons.

### 3. Amazon Prime Video
Amazon retains rights to specific holiday game weeks.
*   **Fixtures:** 20 live matches (two full midweek/bank holiday rounds).
*   **Kickoff Slots:** December bank holidays and early December midweek rounds.
*   **Cost:** £8.99/month (or £95/year for Amazon Prime subscription).
